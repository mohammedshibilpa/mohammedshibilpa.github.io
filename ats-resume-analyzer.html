🤖 AI Resume Analyzer & Recruitment Platform

An AI-powered recruitment platform that helps job seekers optimize resumes and enables recruiters to efficiently screen and rank candidates using ATS scoring, skill matching, and AI-generated interview preparation.

GitHub Features

Project Overview

The AI Resume Analyzer & Recruitment Platform is a dual-mode web application designed for both job seekers and recruiters. Job seekers can upload their resume, compare it with a job description, receive an ATS score, identify missing skills, and obtain AI-generated resume improvement suggestions and interview questions. Recruiters can upload multiple resumes, automatically rank candidates, compare applicants, and analyze candidate skills to streamline the hiring process.
